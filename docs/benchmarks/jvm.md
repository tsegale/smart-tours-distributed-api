## How to read this

The first 10 runs had no GC log ("not logged"). The last 6 (Serial against G1 on tour-core) added one, plus the host's
paging, sampled about every 5 s and compared with each run's measured window:

| tour-core | p95 (ms) | p99 (ms) | Core peak RSS (MiB) | Longest pause (ms) | Full GCs | Host page reads/s in the window, median / max |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Serial | 65.6 | 111.2 | 532 | 31 | 0 | 266 / 1,789 |
| Serial | 43.3 | 73.7 | 526 | 6 | 0 | 1 / 223 |
| Serial | 49.1 | 89.0 | 525 | 18 | 0 | 5 / 563 |
| G1 | 38.6 | 64.6 | 661 | 22 | 0 | 2 / 99 |
| G1 | 41.5 | 58.3 | 666 | 16 | 0 | 1 / 72 |
| G1 | 39.7 | 55.7 | 626 | 26 | 0 | 3 / 31 |

**Memory is the firm result.** Across all runs, tour-core's peak RSS is 525 to 567 MiB on Serial, 626 to 701 on G1 and
764 to 777 on ZGC, against a 768 MiB limit. The gateway's is 275 to 310 MiB on Serial, 281 to 294 on G1 and 528 on
ZGC, against a 512 MiB limit.

**G1's latency edge is real but smaller than the table suggests.** G1's p95 was lower in every run, but its windows
were also the quietest. Against the one Serial run on an equally quiet host (43.3 ms), the gap is 2 to 5 ms at p95 and
9 to 18 ms at p99.

**Single runs mislead here.** The same configuration moved by more than the gap between collectors: Serial/Serial 44
and 146 ms at p95 in the first 10 runs, ZGC/Serial 117 and 43 ms. So did start-up (tour-core on ZGC 24.6 and 15.1 s),
which is why the collectors aren't ranked by it.

## What isn't explained

- **Large pauses on Serial in the first 10 runs.** tour-core logged pauses of 981, 485, 259 and 210 ms in 4 of 6 runs.
  The 981 ms one was in a window with low host paging (page reads 210/s at most, 11:27:58 to 11:29:00), so paging as
  sampled does not explain it. The reruns with GC logs didn't reproduce them: no pause over 31 ms and no full
  collection. Whether they were full collections or host stalls too short for the sampling to catch is not established.
- **ZGC's RSS above the gateway's limit.** 528 MiB in both runs against 512 MiB, with no container killed. An idle check
  showed 369 MiB of RSS, 181 MiB of it shared, against 383 MiB charged to the cgroup, which doesn't account for the
  figure under load. It wasn't pursued, since ZGC wasn't chosen.

To settle latency, repeat this on a machine with memory to spare, such as the Part B server.
